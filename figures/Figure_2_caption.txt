Fig. 2. Patient-grouped nested cross-validation. The development cohort was divided into five fixed patient folds, with all images from each patient kept together. In each outer iteration, four-fold inner cross-validation on the remaining folds determined the loss configurations, patient-prediction aggregation rule, model family, and refit duration. The selected ViT and Swin configurations were refitted on the complete outer-training set using three seeds and used to predict the held-out patients. Predictions were saved without target-label columns. After all five outer iterations had completed, labels were joined and predictions were pooled separately for each method and seed to calculate performance. Metrics were summarized as mean ± sample standard deviation across seeds rather than by ensembling predictions. The primary patient-reference rule was fixed as modal-low, and the official test partition was excluded. The procedure is implemented in Notebook 13.
